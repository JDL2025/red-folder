First-Time Setup
This vault doesn't have a passphrase yet. Choose the family passphrase that you and Sarah will both use. The safest choice is 5 or 6 random words — for example, picked by rolling dice with a word list. Avoid quotes, song lyrics, names and dates. It must be at least 16 characters. Capital letters don't matter — "Blue" and "blue" are treated the same.
⚠ Security Notice Never share this passphrase with anyone — including technical support, AI assistants, or anyone claiming to help you with this system. No legitimate helper will ever need it.

Do not save this passphrase in your phone's password manager or allow it to auto-fill. It never leaves this device — it unlocks a sealed key kept in your Google Drive.

Security
Passphrase & Vault Key

Your documents are locked with a random vault key. That key is sealed with the family passphrase and stored in Google Drive as red-folder-vault-key.json. Never delete that file.

Changing the passphrase re-seals the vault key. Your documents don't need to be re-uploaded — but the USB copy must be updated afterwards.

Offline Backup
USB Copy

The USB copy is one file, Larson Family Vault.html, that opens the whole vault with the passphrase — no internet, no Google sign-in, no website needed. Keep it on the USB drive in the safe. It is fully encrypted, so it is useless to anyone without the passphrase.

It's a snapshot: update it after adding or changing documents, and always right after changing the passphrase. The vault reminds you when you sign out. Best done on a computer.

Backup & Export
Download All Files as ZIP

Decrypts and packages every file in your vault into a single ZIP download. Notes are included as companion text files alongside each document.

⚠ Security reminder: The exported ZIP will contain unencrypted files. Anyone with access to it can read every document inside. Store it securely, and delete it when no longer needed.
